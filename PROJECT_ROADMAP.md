# BDAS Project Roadmap (Post-Vertical Slice Enhancements)

1. **Custom PBR Textures & Satellite Heightmaps**: Upgrade procedural terrain chunks with SRTM Bangladesh elevation grids and runway surface normal maps in OpenGL ES 3.0.
2. **Full 3D Interior Terminals for Regional Airports**: Expand detailed 3D terminal interiors beyond VGHS/VGEG to VGSY, VGCB, VGRJ, VGJR, VGSD, and VGBR.
3. **Live ATC Voice & AI Traffic**: Add scheduled AI domestic traffic flying active airline routes between the 8 Bangladesh airports and interactive ATC clearance callouts.
4. **Multiplayer Formation & Shared Cockpit Missions**: Extend multiplayer sessions to support synchronized emergency challenges and shared leaderboards.
